    Add your tau

    On a tau that already has an address and a published card, run this on its hub:

    tau net universe join

    Your hub signs the request with its own key, so nobody else can list or remove your tau. A line to another tau appears once both of you report each other. tau net universe leave takes your tau off the map. A hosted tau joins from its settings.
