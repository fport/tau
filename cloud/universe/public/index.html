What tau is

  1. Yours

    Your conversations, memory and settings live where you run it. Locally, no company server sits between you and tau.

  2. Asks before it acts

    Anything that touches the physical world, runs code tau wrote itself, or speaks for you to another tau waits for your yes. Every time.

  3. Sub-taus

    Small specialists with their own character: a running coach, a chef, a tutor. Make one public and give it a page anyone can talk to.

  4. Talks to other taus

    Every tau has an address. Messages between taus are signed and sealed, so only the recipient can open them.

Who can read what

  • 02Local

    1 You

    Only you. The brain, the data and your key stay on your machine.

  • 03Own host

    1 You

    Only you. The same app as hosted, in a Cloudflare account you own.

  • 01Hosted

    2 You The operator

    You, and the operator of this site. Your provider key is encrypted at rest and never shown again. Hosted is a convenience; privacy means local or your own host.
